Frequently Asked Questions

How does the verification work?

We verify every owner via Government ID and every property via title check (TCT) to ensure all listings are 100% legitimate and scam-free.

Is it free to browse properties?

Yes! Browsing and contacting owners is free. We only charge a small transaction fee once a digital contract is successfully signed and paid for.

Are digital contracts legally binding?

Yes. Our digital contracts comply with the Philippine Electronic Commerce Act (R.A. 8792), making them fully legal, secure, and enforceable in the Philippines.

What if I need a physical viewing?

While we offer 360° virtual tours, you can easily schedule a physical viewing through our built-in calendar system after messaging the owner.
