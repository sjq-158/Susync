Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

01 Introduction

Welcome to Susync. We are committed to protecting your personal data and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our platform to search for, negotiate, or close property deals in the Philippines.

By using Susync, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy and the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

02 Information We Collect

To provide a secure all-in-one property journey, we collect several types of information:

  • Personal Identification: Full name, date of birth, nationality, and government-issued ID numbers for account verification.
  • Contact Information: Email address, mobile number, and physical address.
  • Financial Data: Bank account details or e-wallet info (GCash/Maya) specifically for secure payment processing.
  • Property Data: Listings, titles, and ownership documents provided by sellers to ensure listing authenticity.
  • Transaction Records: History of negotiations, signed digital contracts, and payment confirmations.

03 How We Use Your Data

Your data enables us to provide a seamless digital real estate experience:

  • To verify the identity of buyers and sellers to prevent fraud and scams.
  • To facilitate direct communication and negotiation between parties.
  • To generate and process digital contracts for property acquisitions.
  • To provide real-time updates on your transaction progress via our tracker.
  • To comply with regulatory requirements from the DICT and HLURB.

💡 Anti-Scam Commitment

We use rigorous verification logic to cross-reference property titles against official records, ensuring every listing on Susync is legitimate.

04 Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including 256-bit SSL encryption for all data transfers. Your sensitive financial information is processed through secure gateways and is never stored unencrypted on our servers.

05 Your Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data. You may also object to processing or request a copy of your data in a portable format.
