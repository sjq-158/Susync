Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the identity verification process take?

›
Typically, the identity verification process is completed within 24 to 48 hours after all required documents (such as government-issued IDs and selfie verification) are submitted.

Can I request a physical viewing after a virtual tour?

›
Absolutely. While 360° virtual tours provide an initial assessment, you can schedule a physical viewing directly with the owner through our built-in messaging system once you are ready for the next step.

Is the ₱8,200,000 price fixed or can I haggle?

›
Many sellers are open to reasonable offers. You can use the "Make an Offer" feature in the direct messaging portal to submit counter-offers and negotiate the final price directly with the owner.

What happens if the seller cancels the deal mid-payment?

›
Susync's integrated payment system protects your funds. If a transaction is cancelled mid-process, our escrow-like framework ensures that funds are refunded or handled according to the terms of the digital contract.
